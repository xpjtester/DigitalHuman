# V1 test — Dutch children and milk

## Spoken line
你知道吗？荷兰的小孩一岁以后，很多就不喝奶粉了，直接开始喝超市里的普通牛奶。

## Visual direction
- 9:16 smartphone selfie
- close head-and-shoulders framing
- outdoor Dutch residential/canal environment
- subtle walking bounce, not cinematic gimbal movement
- natural blinking and occasional glance away from lens
- no presenter pose
- realistic skin texture
- simple white Chinese subtitles in lower third

## Pass criteria
1. Person remains recognizably consistent with authorized reference.
2. Mandarin does not sound like a newsreader.
3. Mouth movement is believable.
4. Clip feels like a real phone vlog rather than an AI studio avatar.
5. No obvious face warping during head turns.

## Test length
5–10 seconds only. Do not scale to 60–90 seconds until this passes.
